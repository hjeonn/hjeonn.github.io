abstract: 'The commercialization of high-energy, high-voltage lithium-ion and next-generation batterieshinges on how precisely the cathode–electrolyte interphase (CEI) can be understood and controlled.Far from being a static boundary, the CEI is a dynamic, compositionally heterogeneous reactionzone in which oxidative decomposition products of the electrolyte accumulate, rearrange, andcontinuously evolve with cycling. This review (i) establishes the fundamentals of CEI formation,structure, and growth kinetics; (ii) surveys interphase behavior across cathode families—Ni-richNCM/NMC, high-voltage spinels, Li-rich oxides, sodium/zinc/dual-ion systems, and solid-statecells; (iii) organizes stabilization strategies spanning electrolyte additives, solvation engineering,surface coating and doping, artificial CEIs, and dry-electrode processing; and (iv) comparesadvanced characterization tools including cryo-EM, in situ TEM, XPS/ToF-SIMS, and EIS/DRT.Crucially, the review then examines how artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML)are reshaping the CEI research paradigm—through machine-learning interatomic potentials,physics-informed neural networks, high-throughput electrolyte and additive screening, thecoupling of multiscale imaging with computational modeling, and data-driven batterymanagement that diagnoses interfacial degradation. We close by articulating an integratedexperiment–simulation–AI vision for the predictive design of the CEI.'
